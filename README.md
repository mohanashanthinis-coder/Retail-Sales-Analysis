# Retail-Sales-Analysis
Retail Sales Performance and Business Insights Dashboard
* Developed an interactive **Microsoft Power BI dashboard** to analyze retail sales performance, customer activity, and inventory insights.
* Transformed raw retail data using **Power Query and DAX**, creating meaningful KPIs, charts, and interactive visualizations.
* Analyzed approximately **₹8.58 million in total sales, 2,000 orders, and 1,000 active customers** to evaluate overall business performance.
* Examined **sales trends, customer activity, product performance, and inventory levels** to identify key business patterns.
* Identified **top-performing products, sales trends, customer segments, and 221 inventory alerts** requiring attention.
* Built interactive dashboards with **KPI cards, charts, slicers, and filters** to support sales, customer, and inventory analysis.
* Generated actionable business insights and strategic recommendations to support **data-driven decision-making, sales growth, customer retention, and inventory planning**.
